# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in BugCode Shield a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sharing exploit payloads or attack instructions against systems you do not own
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues through a private GitHub security advisory or by contacting the maintainers of [BugCodeLabs/BugCodeShield](https://github.com/BugCodeLabs/BugCodeShield). Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that violate this code.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
